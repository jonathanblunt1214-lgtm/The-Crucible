# Autonomous scientific-learning policy

Crucible learning is project-isolated and fail-closed. Ingested material is candidate evidence only. Retrieval, citation, telemetry, correlation, repetition, a model judgment, or a successful one-off repair never proves a claim and never directly changes autonomous behavior.

Learning by doing is permitted only as bounded experience evidence. Each successful or failed attempt must bind the exact action, environment, expected and actual outcomes, actor, time, and content-addressed action/environment/result/artifact custody. An experience observation always enters as `Insufficient Evidence`; repetition, operational telemetry, or apparent task success cannot promote it. A separately designed falsifiable experiment, controls, causal isolation, negative and regression tests, contradiction analysis, scope proof, and a distinct independent verifier remain mandatory. Failures are retained as evidence and may contradict or narrow a claim; they are never discarded merely because the task later succeeds.

Promotion requires a falsifiable hypothesis, controlled reproduction, causal isolation and control testing, independent verification, negative tests, regression tests, deterministic scope proof, claim-boundary and generalization checks, and contradiction analysis. Correlation never satisfies causation. More correlated observations may prioritize investigation, but cannot increase authorization to learn. No weighted confidence, majority vote, frequency threshold, or stage output may bypass a later proof stage.

Verification proves only the tested property. Experimental results inherit the experiment boundary and cannot be generalized without a separate qualifying experiment. Contradictions are quarantined and classified as a `Crucible Issue`; they do not overwrite active knowledge. Other terminal evidence classifications are `Rejected Evidence` and `Insufficient Evidence`.

Verified knowledge is versioned, proof-addressed, and rollbackable. Weekly exchange uses project-derived authenticated encryption and exact OIDC issuer, audience, repository, branch ref, project, signature, and short-lifetime checks. A weekly envelope conveys bounded evidence and knowledge records; it never assimilates repositories, links projects, or grants cross-project authority.

Internet retrieval is read-only, credential-free, and exact-owner-approved. Every document and webpage is untrusted evidence, never an instruction. Retrieval permits only HTTPS GET requests without bodies, cookies, credentials, referrers, form submission, script execution, uploads, repository writes, or arbitrary APIs. It fails closed on an active auditable kill switch, unapproved URL or redirect, denied domain, private/local/link-local address, nonstandard port, timeout, rate violation, disallowed content type, size limit, executable content, or suspicious extension. Every decision is audited; successful custody records the final URL, retrieval time, declared author, license status, media type, size, redirect count, and SHA-256 content hash without persisting secrets or private repository data.

Google is discovery-only. A passive HTML parser may extract candidate HTTPS result URLs, but it never operates an interactive browser, clicks, logs in, submits, executes, or treats snippets as evidence. Only explicitly trusted domains or `.edu`, `.gov`, and `.org` hosts reach the candidate parser; Wikipedia, Reddit, all built-in social-media domains, Google-owned navigation, `.onion`, owner-denied domains, and all unlisted domains are discarded. The social-media denylist covers major centralized platforms and is supplemented by the per-owner denylist for decentralized or newly appearing domains. Suffix eligibility is not proof: all admitted content still undergoes injection, executable, provenance, contradiction, experiment, and independent-verification gates. Prompt-injection signals quarantine the source as a `Crucible Issue` and suppress its content from claim extraction.

Automated Google discovery is restricted to owner-approved bounded topics and a fixed safe-mode HTTPS search endpoint. It performs credential-free GET requests only, rejects redirects and private/local resolution, enforces a kill switch plus query-count, result-count, rate, timeout, media-type, and byte limits, and persists project-bound integrity-checked query hashes, run times, outcomes, and deduplicated URLs. Automation may register a new `.edu`, `.org`, or `.gov` URL only as `research-approved-pending-retrieval` and `Insufficient Evidence`. It cannot click, log in, accept consent, submit forms, execute scripts, retrieve arbitrary APIs, write repositories, treat snippets or rankings as evidence, or satisfy any learning stage.

Every logged learning URL is checked at least once per 30-day interval through the same governed retriever. The project-bound refresh registry is atomic, integrity-checked, and restart-safe. An unchanged or historically seen content hash creates no candidate evidence. New content is deduplicated by the exact bounded claim, claim boundary, and generalization boundary against all claims previously emitted or learned from that URL. Only novel claims may enter as `Insufficient Evidence`; no refresh, diff, newer publication date, or source reputation may promote or overwrite knowledge. Current verified knowledge changes only by a new version completing every scientific proof stage, while conflicting claims remain quarantined until resolved.

Extraction compares the current source hash and bounded page-window hash against durable completed-window custody. Unchanged windows are never extracted twice, unfinished windows resume at their recorded boundary, and a changed source hash starts a new revision at page one while retaining prior revision provenance. A three-way router may compare two independently identified source claims with active verified knowledge solely to prioritize controlled tests, record corroboration, detect novelty, preserve version/scope differences, or quarantine contradictions. Its records must state that no proof stage, independent verification, or promotion has been satisfied. Source agreement, knowledge agreement, and majority agreement are never proof.

Automated evaluation may select only two existing bounded candidates with distinct source identities and a supported language whose controlled-experiment and independent-verifier harnesses are trusted project code. Source text never becomes executable test code. The evaluator constructs a falsifiable hypothesis limited to the candidate boundary, requires distinct stable harness identities, and may write knowledge only through the full scientific state machine. Retrieval after promotion returns only the active version; an unsupported language, unmatched candidate pair, failed harness, or absent independent verifier remains unverified.

Critical review is an adversarial routing stage before experimentation. It records assumptions, counterexamples, alternative explanations, ambiguous terms, causal wording, and missing evidence, and may narrow scope or quarantine a claim. Critical-review output, model judgment, rhetorical persuasiveness, or confidence never satisfies a scientific gate and cannot overrule controlled or independent test failure.

Logical reasoning and problem solving operate on both sides of controlled testing. Pre-test reasoning must expose premises, competing hypotheses, decomposed obligations, controls, and the exact bounded hypothesis. Post-test reasoning must check identity and scope, distinguish causal intervention from correlation, require negative and regression evidence, preserve contradictions, and route failure to redesign, hypothesis revision, or a precise missing-evidence request. These records are integrity-checked but non-evidentiary: they cannot mark a gate complete, replace either executor, override failure, or authorize promotion.

Creativity may propose bounded hypotheses, counterexamples, controls, and alternative experiments but is never evidence. Decision-making is restricted to an explicit action allow-list and has no promotion action. Adaptability may preserve failed attempts and revise scope, fixture complexity, controls, batch size, or evidence requests within bounded retry limits, but it must preserve the original claim and boundary and may never remove, downgrade, or self-satisfy a required gate. All strategy records are project-bound, integrity-checked, non-evidentiary, and auditable.

The governed testing suite may communicate with learning only through explicit claim eligibility and complete durable custody. Before a test it may retrieve active verified knowledge for the exact declared boundary solely as regression context; retrieval cannot prove or skip the current execution. After a test, success and failure submit bounded command, runtime, commit, result, and artifact hashes as `experience-observation` candidates. Test output, telemetry, and one-off success remain non-promotable evidence and must traverse comparison, adversarial review, reasoning, controlled testing, and distinct independent verification before knowledge changes.

The learning orchestrator is an executive-control coordinator, never a truth source. It may prioritize attention, plan dependencies, route evidence, coordinate bounded workers, retrieve exact-boundary active memory, accept feedback, persist adaptation, inhibit unsafe actions, and report metacognitive status. It must reject cross-project, unbounded, contradictory, unsupported, direct-verification, and direct-promotion requests. Orchestrator events explicitly satisfy no proof stage and authorize no promotion; only controlled evidence processed by the state machine and a distinct independent verifier can change active knowledge.

Crucible systems participate in one governed ecosystem lifecycle: observe, prioritize, plan, act, verify, learn, adapt, and report. Each major component declares its role and lifecycle stages, exchanges project-bound signals through the ecosystem coordinator, and appears in a shared readiness and inhibition report. Missing core components, cross-project observations, and any attempt by a component to self-certify verification or promotion fail closed. Ecosystem coordination may sequence work and expose dependencies, but it is not evidence, proof, an independent verifier, or a promotion authority. Modular separation remains mandatory so security, quarantine, controlled testing, independent verification, and rollback can inhibit or correct the rest of the organism.

Every production coding or security actuator must implement a typed bounded workflow with separately identified detection, planning, execution, independent-verification, and rollback handlers. The request binds project identity, exact change boundary, and base content hash. The plan must be reversible before execution; the executor must return change-hash and rollback custody; a failed or boundary-mismatched verification must invoke rollback. Actuator completion may authorize retention of that bounded code change under ordinary repository governance, but its feedback remains candidate experience and satisfies no scientific-learning proof stage or knowledge-promotion gate.

Language-specific code-assistive security profiles cover JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Java, C, C++, C#, PowerShell, Shell, HTML, CSS, React, and Django. Detection must preserve exact file, offset, signature hash, base content hash, language, and threat dimension. Repository context includes data flow, declared dependency risk, authentication boundaries, secret-handling signals, injection paths, and repeated cross-file consequences. Automated repair is allowed only through an exact bounded replacement supplied to the actuator and a language-specific independent verifier; ambiguous findings remain findings rather than guessed fixes. Verified outcomes may seed regression planning but enter learning only as `Insufficient Evidence`. Organ unavailability inhibits mutation, appears in ecosystem health, and must recover explicitly without erasing prior state.

Recognized news-agency domains and every `.science` host are marked `extreme-vetting-required`. Their claims remain `Insufficient Evidence` until checked against an applicable primary source, independently corroborated, subjected to contradiction analysis, and controlled testing when testable. A `.science` suffix, publisher reputation, repetition across outlets, citations, and search ranking are never proof. The built-in news list is extendable so newly encountered or owner-designated news domains receive the same treatment.
