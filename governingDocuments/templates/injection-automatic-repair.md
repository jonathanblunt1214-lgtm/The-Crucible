# Universal Injection Automatic Repair Rule

During an active authorized injection/assimilation, a failing required Crucible or receiving-project native validation result is not a stopping point by itself.

When the exact failure is safe, technically resolvable, within the active injection authority, and not blocked by an unresolved governance conflict, the injector must continue directly through evidence collection, diagnosis, repair on the authorized development path, and retest until the required assimilation validation passes or a genuine blocker is reached.

The injector must never use this continuation rule to widen authority, touch production directly, weaken or bypass a gate, suppress a genuine finding, invent business logic, expose secrets, force-push, or overwrite valid concurrent work.

Every future injection package must preserve this rule or a stricter equivalent.
